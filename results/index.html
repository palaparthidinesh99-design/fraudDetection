How to Read and Interpret These Results

In imbalanced fraud detection (0.17% fraud rate), standard metrics like Accuracy (99.9%) are misleading because a trivial classifier predicting non-fraud achieves 99.83% accuracy while failing 100% of frauds. The paper introduces TCS (Total Cost Saved) and ACM (Average Cost Minimization) to measure real financial dollars preserved. Cost-sensitive oversamplers weight synthetic instances by transaction amount, guaranteeing fraud catch rates where fraudulent dollar amounts are largest.

💡 Why Cost-Sensitive Oversampling Dominates

Standard SMOTE creates synthetic points uniformly across the minority class, ignoring financial consequences. If an algorithm catches ten $5 frauds but misses a single $5,000 fraud, standard accuracy and recall view this as 90% accuracy, while the business suffers a devastating loss. Cost-Sensitive SMOTE, ADASYN, and Borderline variants synthesize points proportionally to fraud transaction dollar amounts, forcing decision boundaries around high-value fraudulent transactions.
